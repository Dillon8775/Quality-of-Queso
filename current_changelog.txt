Version 1.5 (MC Fabric 1.21.9-1.21.11, Forge 1.20.1 and 1.21.11):
Main changes:
- Added a keybind to hide the recipe search book, by pressing CTRL + the keybind (default = R).

Other changes:
- Hotbar slots are grayed out if the player hovers over the "include/exclude hotbar" button, and are also grayed out if CTRL is held down.
- Holding "shift" over a stack in a container will grayout hotbar slots if excluding hotbar.
- Hotbar slots are now grayed out even if a transfer button is inactive but hovered.
- The "1" only appears over the Quick Drop button if it's hovered.
- Optimized language (again).
