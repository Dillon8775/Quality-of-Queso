- Added container searching and transferring (toggleable)!
- At the top of any chest or shulker box, you can now search for any item and it will highlight the selected item!
- You can also use "#" in the beginning of your search query to search for item tags.
- You can now transfer items from chest -> inventory, or inventory -> chest.
- To transfer items from inventory -> chest, you must be holding down alt. This is to prevent from pressing the button and sending your whole inventory into the chest by accident.
- Only compatible with Minecraft 1.21.4 and 1.21.5.
- Bug fixes and optimizations.
