Version 1.5.1 (MC Fabric 1.21.11, Forge 1.20.1 and 1.21.11):
Armor Status:
- Added "Armor Status" feature, which displays your armor HUD beside your hotbar, allowing you to see it's durability.
- "ALWAYS" - Always show armor.
- "ON CHANGE" - Show armor when it's durability is changed or item is changed.
- "OFF" - Don't show armor.

Item Count:
- See how many of the item that you are holding in your inventory, which displays beside your hotbar.
- Configuration options:
- We will use ender pearls for this example.
- "TOTAL" - display the total amount of ender pearls in your inventory.
- "STACKS" - displays the amount of perfect stacks you have (ex. "5 x64", meaning 5 stacks of ender pearls). If amount of stacks aren't perfect then it just displays the total amount.
- "REMAINDER" - displays the amount of perfect stacks you have (ex. "7 x64") + any additional stacks you have (final result: "7x64 & 12", meaning 7 stacks and 12 additional ender pearls).

- You can find "Armor Status" and "Item Count" configuration settings in the "HUD Options...".

Drag-Sorting Changes:
- In v1.5, drag-sorting was introduced, where you can hold ALT to exclude slots.
- With this, you can now hold ALT+SHIFT, and you can manually pick which slots to transfer/search (so it will exclude all slots, except the ones that you pick).
- You can now save your "excluded slots"! Just enable the "Save Excluded Slots" option, and when you re-open a container of that size, your excluded slots will be restored.

"Move Items If":
- You can now move items if an item is matching what is being moved, and this is configurable.
- "CONTAINER ISN'T FULL" (this is what it was previously): as long as every slot in the container isn't filled, allow moving.
- "LESS THAN MAX ITEM COUNT": if the item being moved + the slot it's being moved to is less than the max item count, allow moving.
- "CAN MOVE AT ALL": if the slot being moved to's stack is less than 64, allow moving.
- This feature is a little confusing, but just trust me. It helps a lot when transferring large containers.

Mob hit ding:
- Plays the ding sound effect when you hit a mob from at least 15 blocks away (toggleable).

Remove fog:
- Added the option to remove fog! (yes)
- If you run into issues with the fog feature (like mod compatibility), go into the universal quality of queso configuration .json file, and set "applyFogFunction" to false.
- The fog feature will not work if the Simple Keybinds mod is loaded. You will need to use the keybind in Simple Keybinds if you combine these mods.

Other changes:
- Added the transfer button to brewing stand, so you can move all potions out at once.
- You can hold "SHIFT" on the done button in the Quality of Queso configuration screen to open your configuration directory.

- Added path to forge for configuration screen (click "Mods" on Forge title screen/pause screen, find Quality of Queso and configure from there).

Bug fixes:
- Fixed bug where if drag sorting and holding ALT, you can still quick equip.
- Fixed 1.20.1 bug where inventory management buttons do not display properly when searching.
- Fixed other Forge 1.20.1 bugs.
