Version 1.5.5 (MC Fabric 1.21.11, Forge 1.20.1 and 1.21.11):
- Using any inventory management button now plays bundle sounds instead of clicking sounds.

- Renamed "Better Searching" to "Quick Search"
- "Better Searching (now Quick Search)" option now applies to chests and inventories.
- Renamed "Drag-To-Sort" to "Drag Sorting".
- Renamed "Better GUI Exit" to "Quick GUI Exit".

- "Prevent E from typing" is now enabled by default.
- Fixed bug with "Prevent E from typing" and "Better Searching" causing weird screen opening/closing.

- Added "Always Prevent Rage Quitting" option, to force-enable "Prevent Rage Quitting" whenever you join a server.

- Improved compatibility with the Speedrunner Mod.
- A few other optimizations.

- Fixed bug with Multi-Server Configs where attempting to save options in first join re-loads the config, so no options save.
- Multi-Server configs now work as intended.
- Fixed slight incompatibility with the Flashback mod (one of the QoQ buttons overlaps the pause button).
- Fixed crash with mob hit ding.
